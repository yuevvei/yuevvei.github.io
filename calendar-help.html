Subscribe to your team calendar

Add your team's games, practices, and meetings to the calendar app you already use. Events stay up to date automatically — you never have to re-add them.

First, get your link: open Court Camper, go to your team's Info tab, find Team Calendar, and tap Subscribe to Calendar. Then follow the steps for your calendar app below.

Keep your link private. Anyone who has it can see your team's schedule. If you share it by accident, tap Reset link in the app — the old link stops working immediately.

📱 iPhone or iPad

  1. In Court Camper, tap Add to Calendar.
  2. Your Calendar app opens and asks if you want to subscribe. Tap Subscribe.
  3. Choose a colour and which account to add it to, then tap Add.

To change how often it refreshes: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Accounts → Subscribed Calendars, pick the calendar, and set Refresh to every 15 minutes or hourly.

🖥️ Mac

  1. Copy your link from Court Camper.
  2. Open the Calendar app and choose File → New Calendar Subscription.
  3. Paste the link, click Subscribe, then set Auto-refresh to Every hour.

📅 Google Calendar

This one has to be done on a computer. The Google Calendar mobile app cannot add calendar subscriptions. Add it once on a desktop browser and it will then show up in the mobile app automatically, signed in to the same Google account.
  1. Copy your link from Court Camper (email or message it to yourself to get it onto your computer).
  2. On a computer, open calendar.google.com.
  3. In the left sidebar, next to Other calendars, click + and choose From URL.
  4. Paste the link and click Add calendar.

📧 Outlook

  1. Copy your link from Court Camper.
  2. Open outlook.com and go to Calendar.
  3. Click Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  4. Paste the link, give the calendar a name, and click Import.

⏱️ Why don't changes show up right away?

Subscribed calendars are not updated instantly. Your calendar app decides how often to check for changes, and Court Camper cannot speed that up:

  • Apple Calendar — as often as every 15 minutes, if you change the refresh setting.
  • Google Calendar — usually every 8 to 24 hours. This cannot be changed.
  • Outlook — usually every few hours.
Always trust the app first. For last-minute schedule changes, Court Camper and its push notifications are the source of truth. Your subscribed calendar is a convenience, not a replacement.

🔁 Other things to know

  • Cancelled events stay on your calendar with CANCELLED in the title, so you can see that something was called off.
  • You can't RSVP from your calendar app. The subscription is read-only — open Court Camper to respond to events.
  • One link per team. If you follow more than one team, subscribe to each team's calendar separately from that team's Info tab.
  • Leaving a team stops its calendar. The subscription stops updating once you are no longer on the team; you can remove it from your calendar app.
