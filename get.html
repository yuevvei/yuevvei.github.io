Joining a team

  1. Install the app and create an account with your email address.
  2. Tap Join a Team and enter the invite code you were given.
  3. That is it — your team's schedule, roster, chat, and photos are waiting.

Invite codes are issued by your team's coach or manager. If you do not have one, ask them to send you an invite.
